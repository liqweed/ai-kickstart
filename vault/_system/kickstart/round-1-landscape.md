# Round 1 — Getting to know you

**Time:** ~15–20 min · **Feeds:** `_system/profile/LANDSCAPE.md` and the shortlist of areas for [[round-2-areas|Round 2]]

**Opening** (adapt to your voice):
> "Hi! I'm going to ask you about 9 short questions so I can get a feel for your life and setup. Nothing technical needed. Answer as briefly or as rambly as you like, skip anything, and say 'pause' whenever. Ready?"

## Questions

Ask in this order. Adapt the wording and skip what's already known.

1. **What made you curious?** What would make this worth your time? *Why: so I aim at what matters to you.*
   *Examples: "less mental load", "get on top of admin", "be better at my job", "more time with family", "just curious what's possible".*

2. **Life snapshot.** Who's in your household, and what's daily life like? *Why: so suggestions fit your actual life.*
   *Prompts if they need them: partner? kids (ages roughly)? pets? Living alone, with family, with housemates? Which languages at home? City-level location is plenty.*

3. **Work, in plain words.** What do you do, and what's a typical week like? *Why: AI can help at work in very different ways depending on the job.*
   *Prompts: employed / self-employed / student / between things / retired; office, remote, on the road, hands-on; lots of email? meetings? writing? numbers? people? Keep anything confidential out.*

4. **Rhythm of the week.** When do your days start and end? Which days are packed and which are calmer? *Why: to time briefings and reviews when they'd actually help.*

5. **Devices.** What do you use day to day? *Why: some tools only work on some systems.*
   *Choices: Mac · Windows PC · Linux · Chromebook · iPhone · Android · iPad/tablet · work laptop (locked down?)*
   Follow-up if relevant: *"Can you install apps on your work computer, or is it managed by IT?"*

6. **Apps you already live in.** Where do your email, calendar, to-dos, notes and files live? *Why: we'll build around what you already use, not replace it.*
   *Examples: Gmail/Google Calendar · Outlook/Microsoft 365 · iCloud/Apple apps · Todoist/Things/Microsoft To Do · paper notebook · WhatsApp/Signal/Telegram · Google Drive/OneDrive/Dropbox · a smart-home app.*

7. **AI so far.** Which AI tools have you tried, and how did it go? *Why: to start from where you are.*
   *Examples: "never really", "ChatGPT for emails", "Claude for writing", "I use it daily", "tried it, didn't stick".*

8. **Comfort with tech, 1–5.** *Why: so I explain things at the right level.*
   1 = "I'd rather not touch settings" · 2 = "I can install an app if guided" · 3 = "I'm comfortable figuring things out" · 4 = "I've used a terminal / spreadsheets with formulas" · 5 = "I write code or scripts".
   Also: **time budget.** Roughly how much time per week would you happily spend setting this up? *(e.g. 20 min, an hour, "a weekend to get going")*

9. **Privacy lines.** Is there anything you'd rather keep away from AI entirely? *Why: so we respect it from day one.*
   *Examples: health, finances, kids' details, work documents, other people's information. "Nothing in particular" is fine. Note: we never store passwords or ID numbers regardless.*

## After the questions

- **Reflect back** a 6–8 bullet snapshot. Ask "anything to fix?"
- **Draft `LANDSCAPE.md`** from the answers. Show it, then save it once they're happy.
- **Build the Round 2 shortlist.** Using [[function-catalog]], pick the **areas** (not individual functions) that plausibly fit this person. Examples: no kids → drop Kids. Self-employed → add Business admin. Comfort 1–2 → hold back the Builder track for now. Write them under *Signals for next round*.
- **Note tone signals**, e.g. "prefers short questions", "joked a lot, keep it light", "time-poor, keep rounds to 10 min".
- Save `answers/round-1-YYYY-MM-DD.md`, tick **P1.1** in the harness plan, and hand off:
  > "Next time, Round 2: *where could help feel good?* About 20 minutes. Start it with: **'Start round 2 of my kickstart interview.'**"
