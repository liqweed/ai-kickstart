# Round 5 — Check-in (repeatable)

**When:** about 2 weeks after Round 4, then monthly, or whenever things feel off · **Time:** ~10 min · **Reads:** harness plan session log, recent briefings/reviews, previous check-ins · **Feeds:** harness plan, `LEARNED.md`, `PREFERENCES.md`

**Opening:**
> "Quick check-in, about 7 questions. The goal is to keep what works, fix what annoys you, and drop what you don't use."

## Questions

1. **Used and loved.** What did you actually use, and what helped most? *(Prompt with what the session log shows was built.)*
2. **Built but unused.** What did we build that you haven't touched? Be honest. Unused things should be fixed or deleted, not kept out of guilt.
3. **Friction.** What annoyed you? Too long, wrong tone, asked too much, asked too little, wrong time of day?
4. **Life changes.** Anything new? New job, new project, new season, new device, someone new in your life?
5. **New wishes.** Anything you found yourself wishing it could do?
6. **Pace.** Is the pace of building right? Faster, slower, pause for a while?
7. **Habits.** How did the two habits from the *Tips & Insights* note go (see the harness Decisions log)? Keep, swap, or drop?

## After the questions

- Turn friction into **concrete changes**: edit `PREFERENCES.md`, edit or retire a skill, move a schedule. Show changes first.
- Turn new wishes into **backlog items** (same format as Round 3).
- Add 1–3 lines to `LEARNED.md` → *Working with AI*.
- Re-order the harness plan if priorities shifted. Retire unused skills to `99-archive/`, after asking.
- If no app is connected yet and they're past P3, mention P4.0 (*"What could I connect?"*): it's often where the setup starts to feel useful.
- Save `answers/round-5-YYYY-MM-DD.md` and add a session-log entry in the harness plan.
- Suggest the next check-in date.
