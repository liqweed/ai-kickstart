# Round 3 — Deep dives

**Time:** ~10 min per area (split across sessions if you like) · **Reads:** Rounds 1–2 · **Feeds:** the *Personal backlog* in `_system/HARNESS-PLAN.md`

**Opening:**
> "Let's make your top areas concrete. For each one I'll ask how it works today and what 'great' would look like. We'll start with <top area>. Want to do all of them today, or one at a time?"

## Questions per area

Pick the 5–7 most useful questions for the area. Don't ask all of them by rote.

1. **Last time.** Walk me through the last time you did this. What happened, step by step? *(Stories beat abstractions.)*
2. **Who's involved?** Just you, or also a partner, kids, colleagues, clients? Who decides?
3. **Where does the information live?** Email, calendar, an app, paper, your head, a website you check, a school portal, a spreadsheet?
4. **Musts and nevers.** Anything that's non-negotiable? *Examples: dietary needs, budget ceiling, "must have English subtitles", "never book without asking", "only weekends", accessibility.* These become **hard rules** in the skill.
5. **What does "great" look like?** If this were handled brilliantly, what would you see, and when? *Examples: "a message on Sunday evening with the week's plan", "three options, one recommendation", "a checklist I can tick".*
6. **How often, and when?** On demand, or on a schedule (daily, weekly, before an event)?
7. **How hands-on should the AI be?** *Choose:* **Suggest** (ideas only) · **Draft** (prepares, I review and send) · **Do, then tell me** (only for safe, reversible things). Money, messages to people and anything irreversible stay at Suggest or Draft.
8. **Quick win vs. full version.** What would be useful *this week*, even if rough?

## Turning answers into backlog items

For each area, write **1–3 backlog items** into the harness plan's *Personal backlog* table:

| Field | How to fill it |
|---|---|
| ID | `B1`, `B2`, … |
| Area | The area name |
| Idea | One line, outcome-first: "Sunday evening family-week plan" |
| Value | 1–3 from Round 2 |
| Effort | **S** = a skill using only the vault and web search · **M** = needs a connection to an app (calendar, email…) · **L** = needs a script or a schedule |
| Phase | S → P2 (first wins) · M → P4 (connections) · L → P5/P6 (scripts, automations) |
| Status | `todo` |

Also note, per item: data sources, hard rules (musts/nevers), the shape of the output, cadence, and autonomy level. That's everything a later session needs to build the skill without asking again. Put these notes in the answer file under a `## Specs` heading, one sub-heading per item.

## After the questions

- Reflect back the backlog items. Ask: *"If you could only have one of these next week, which?"* Mark it ★ in the backlog.
- Save `answers/round-3-YYYY-MM-DD.md` (or `round-3b-…` for later sessions), tick **P1.3** once every top area is done, and hand off:
  > "Last round next: *how you like to work with AI*. That's tone, how much I should do on my own, and what's off-limits. Then I'll put everything together. Start with: **'Start round 4 of my kickstart interview.'**"
