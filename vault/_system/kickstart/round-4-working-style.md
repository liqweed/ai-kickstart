# Round 4 — How you like to work with AI (+ synthesis)

**Time:** ~15 min of questions + ~10 min of synthesis · **Reads:** Rounds 1–3 · **Feeds:** `PREFERENCES.md`, `MISSION.md`, `GOALS.md`, `PROJECTS.md`, the harness plan

**Opening:**
> "Last round! A few questions about how you'd like me to behave. Then I'll pull everything together into your profile and suggest your first three quick wins."

## Questions

1. **Talk to me like…** *Choose or describe:* short and direct · warm and chatty · like a coach · like a colleague · "explain like I'm new to this". Any language preference?
2. **Length.** Answer first and details on request, or the full picture up front?
3. **Formats you like.** *Examples:* tables · checklists · three options + a recommendation · a short summary then details · voice-friendly plain text.
4. **Do without asking.** Which of these can I just do? *(Show the defaults in `PREFERENCES.md` → "Do without asking". Let them add or remove.)*
5. **Always ask first.** Here's the default "ask first" list (deleting, sending, spending, installing, connecting accounts, editing your profile). Anything to add?
6. **Hard nevers.** Anything I should never do, store or suggest?
7. **Pushback.** Should I challenge you when something conflicts with your goals, or only when asked?
8. **Rhythm.** Would a **daily briefing** help? When? A **weekly review**? Which day and time? *(Fine to say no to both.)*
9. **Principles.** Any principles you'd like my advice to respect? *Examples: "family first", "frugal by default", "quality over quantity", "health is non-negotiable".* This seeds `MISSION.md`.
10. **Where do reminders reach you?** Calendar, a to-do app, phone notifications, email, or "I'll check my notes"?

## Synthesis (do this right after the questions)

1. **Draft all profile files** that are still empty or thin: `PREFERENCES.md` (from this round), `MISSION.md` (principles, a good year, anti-goals), `GOALS.md` (refine from Round 2), `PROJECTS.md` (anything in flight that came up). Show each draft briefly and save once approved. **They have the final word.**
2. **Finalise the harness plan.** Read `_system/HARNESS-PLAN.md`:
   - Fill in the P2 placeholders (P2.3–P2.5) with the **three best first wins**: high value, effort **S**, one per top area where possible. The ★ item from Round 3 goes first.
   - Remove or mark `[-]` any generic steps that don't fit them. Examples: no daily briefing wanted, or a locked-down work laptop.
   - Make sure every backlog item from Round 3 is in the *Personal backlog* table.
   - Low tech comfort, or a chat-only AI? Mark P0.6 `[-]` (the tools are optional). Locked-down work laptop? Note it next to P4.
3. **Present the plan** in plain words:
   > "Here's your setup in one screen: *who I think you are* (3 lines), *your first three wins* (with when you'll feel them), and *what comes after* (connections, automations), at whatever pace you like."
4. Save `answers/round-4-YYYY-MM-DD.md`, tick **P1.4** and **P1.5**, and hand off:
   > "From now on, whenever you have 15 minutes, just say **'Continue my harness plan'** and I'll do the next step with you. In about two weeks I'll suggest a short check-in (Round 5)."
