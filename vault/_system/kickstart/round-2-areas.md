# Round 2 — Where could help feel good?

**Time:** ~20 min · **Reads:** Round 1 answers + `LANDSCAPE.md` · **Feeds:** ranked areas for [[round-3-deep-dives|Round 3]], first draft of `GOALS.md`

**Opening:**
> "Last time I got a picture of your life. Today is about where AI could actually make a difference. First some open questions, then I'll show you a menu and you pick. About 8 questions."

## Part A — open questions (before showing any menu)

Ask these first, so the menu doesn't put ideas in their head.

1. **A good year.** Picture twelve months from now and it was a really good year. What happened? *Why: so we aim at what you care about, not just at chores.*
2. **Energy drains.** Which tasks do you dread, put off, or find draining? *Examples: admin letters, email backlog, meal planning, chasing appointments, expense reports, planning holidays, school logistics.*
3. **Things that slip.** What falls through the cracks? *Examples: birthdays, renewals and cancellations, follow-ups, warranty claims, replying to friends, household maintenance.*
4. **Repeat performances.** What do you do over and over, weekly or monthly, roughly the same way each time?
5. **Magic wand.** If you had a brilliant, tireless assistant for an hour a day, what would you hand over first?
6. **More of.** What would you love to do *more* of, if you had the time or the push? *Examples: reading, learning a language, hobbies, date nights, exercise, seeing friends, a side project.*

## Part B — the menu

7. **Show the tailored menu.** Use the area shortlist from Round 1's *Signals*. For each area, show 3–5 example functions from [[function-catalog]] in one line each. Mark the ones that echo what they said in Part A with ★. Ask: *"Which of these make you think 'yes, that'*? Pick as many as you like."
   - If they have the kit's HTML guide, they can also tick boxes on the **catalog page** and paste the generated list.
8. **Rank.** From their picks, help them choose a **top 3–5 areas**. For each, ask two quick ratings:
   - **Pain or value:** 1 = nice to have · 2 = would help · 3 = would genuinely change my week
   - **How often:** daily · weekly · monthly · occasionally

## After the questions

- Reflect back: the top areas in rank order, with the reason in their words.
- Draft the **"Now"** and **"This year"** sections of `GOALS.md` from the "good year" and "more of" answers. Show it before saving.
- In *Signals for next round*, list each top area and the functions they starred, plus guesses about where the data lives (email? paper? an app?), to check in Round 3.
- Save `answers/round-2-YYYY-MM-DD.md`, tick **P1.2**, and hand off:
  > "Next: Round 3, a short deep dive into each of your top areas, about 10 minutes per area. We can do them all at once or one per session. Start with: **'Start round 3 of my kickstart interview.'**"
