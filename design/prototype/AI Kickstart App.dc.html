What this app keeps, and where

A small folder in your home folder, ~/.ai-kickstart/, holding:

  • where your vault is
  • which kit version it has
  • a fingerprint of each kit file as installed, so the app can tell which ones you've changed
  • whether update checks are on, and the last version you saw
  • which AI you chose

Nothing from your notes is copied there. Your progress is read from your vault each time.

This forgets where your vault is and resets these settings. Your vault and every note in it stay exactly where they are.
