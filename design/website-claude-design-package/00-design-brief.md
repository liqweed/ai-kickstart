# 00 · Design brief: ai-kickstart.me (website)

**AI Kickstart** is a free, open-source (CC BY 4.0) kit that helps an ordinary person start working with AI across their personal life, family, home and work. It gives their AI a place to live (an Obsidian vault of plain notes they own), a memory of them (a profile built through a friendly multi-round interview), playbooks ("skills"), and a step-by-step plan they progress through over many short sessions.

The website at **ai-kickstart.me** is how people discover it, understand it, get it, and come back to it during setup.

## Binding (do not deviate)

- **Voice.** Warm, plain, second person, short sentences. Explain any technical term the first time it appears. Not hype, not salesy, not "AI-powered" sparkle. Source: the kit's own writing rules (`reference-specs/kit-facts.md` §Voice).
- **Credit line, verbatim:** "Designed and shared by Ophir Radnitz". It must appear on the site, and it is the only personal detail anywhere.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in **both** light and dark mode (both are required; follow the OS setting). Meaning is never carried by colour alone. Full keyboard operation with a visible focus state. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Privacy is part of the product.** No analytics, trackers, cookies, accounts or third-party requests (fonts, CDNs, embeds). This is also a visible promise the site makes.
- **Facts are fixed.** Names, counts, rounds, phases, catalog contents and prompts come from `03-target-content.md`. Render them; don't invent new ones.

## Aesthetic direction (spirit, not spec)

- It should feel like a **gift from a thoughtful friend**: calm, generous, trustworthy, a little crafted. It shouldn't feel like a startup landing page or a developer tool.
- The audience includes people who are wary of AI. Nothing should feel intimidating, futuristic-for-its-own-sake or surveillance-adjacent.
- The kit's ideas are **plain files you own**, **small steps over time**, and **you stay in charge**. If the visual language can carry some of that, great.
