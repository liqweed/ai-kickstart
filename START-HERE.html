What's in this folder

  • vault/ is the heart of the kit: a folder of plain notes that becomes your AI's memory. You'll open it in Obsidian, a free notes app.
  • README.md is a short overview, also written for an AI that reads the kit on your behalf.
  • This page. Nothing else to install from here.

The short version

If you'd rather not open the website, this is all of Day 1. About an hour.

  1. Install Obsidian from obsidian.md. It's free.
  2. Give your vault a home. Move the vault folder out of this kit to somewhere permanent and backed up, and rename it, for example Documents/My Vault. In Obsidian, choose Open folder as vault and pick it.
  3. Connect your AI. Use an AI app that can read and write files in a folder you choose, and choose your vault folder (only that one). Only have a chat app? It works too, with some copy-paste: the website's setup page shows which files to give it.
  4. Say hello. Paste this into your AI:
First contact
Hi! This folder is my new Obsidian vault, set up from the AI Kickstart kit.
Please read AGENTS.md and _system/index.md (nothing else yet), then tell me in three short lines:
1) what this vault is for, 2) how you'll help me, 3) what we should do first.
Then create a test note at _inbox/hello.md that says "It works!" so I can check that you can write here.

If _inbox/hello.md appears in Obsidian, it works. Delete it yourself.

After that, one sentence

Start every session, in any AI tool, with this. The plan in your vault (_system/HARNESS-PLAN.md) remembers where you were and leads from here: backup and privacy first, then a friendly interview, then your first wins.

The main one
Continue my harness plan.

Put in your notes only what you'd be comfortable with your AI provider processing. Passwords, card numbers and ID numbers never go in.
