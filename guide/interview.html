Week 1 · four short conversations

The interview

Before suggesting anything, the AI gets to know you: your life, your work, your devices, what drains you and what you'd love more of. It's friendly, one question at a time, and every round builds on the one before.

How it feels

☝️

One question at a time

Never a wall of questions. You'll always see roughly where you are: "Round 1 · question 3 of 9".

🎛️

Examples for everything

Every question comes with options to react to. Reacting is easier than inventing.

🎤

Ramble away

Use voice dictation and talk it out. Turning your words into structure is the AI's job.

⏸️

Skip or pause anytime

"Skip" is always fine. Say "pause" and it saves your answers and picks up next time.

🪞

It reflects back

Every few answers you'll hear "So far I've got… right?" You correct anything that's off.

🔒

Vague is fine

It never pushes for details. City-level, first names, "keep it vague": all good.

The rounds

1

Getting to know you

~15–20 min · 9 questions
  • What made you curious, and what would make this worth it
  • Household and life stage · work in plain words · weekly rhythm
  • Devices and operating systems · apps you already live in
  • AI so far · comfort with tech (1–5) · time budget · privacy lines
your landscape profile + a shortlist of areas that fit you
2

Where could help feel good?

~20 min · open questions first, then a menu
  • A good year looks like… · energy drains · things that slip
  • Repeat performances · the magic-wand question · what you'd love more of
  • Then a menu tailored to Round 1. Pick, then rank your top 3–5 areas
ranked areas + a first draft of your goals
3

Deep dives

~10 min per top area · one sitting or several
  • "Walk me through the last time you did this"
  • Who's involved · where the information lives · musts and nevers
  • What "great" looks like · how often · how hands-on the AI should be
concrete backlog items in your harness plan, each ready to build
4

How you like to work with AI (+ synthesis)

~15 min questions + ~10 min synthesis
  • Tone, length, formats · what it may just do · what it must ask first · hard nevers
  • Daily briefing? Weekly review? When? · principles to respect
  • Then: your full profile drafted for your approval, plus your first three wins
all profile files + the finished harness plan
5

Check-in (repeatable)

~10 min · two weeks after setup, then monthly
  • Used and loved · built but unused (be honest!) · friction
  • Life changes · new wishes · is the pace right?
fixes, pruning, new backlog items, lessons learned
How rounds feed each other Each round saves a short answer file with a hidden section called Signals for next round: what to explore, what to skip, tone notes. The next round reads all earlier files first. If you don't have kids, you'll never be asked about school logistics. If you lit up about cooking, Round 3 will dig in.

Using a plain chat app?

It still works. At the end of each round the AI gives you paste-ready blocks, each headed with a file path such as _system/kickstart/answers/round-1-2026-10-14.md or _system/profile/LANDSCAPE.md. Paste each into Obsidian. Before the next round, attach the earlier answer files.

Chat-only round start
Using the attached kickstart files (interview-protocol.md and the round files), run round 1 of my kickstart interview in chat-only mode: one friendly question at a time, and at the end give me paste-ready markdown blocks headed with their file paths.

Tips for a great interview

  • Do it somewhere comfortable, with a coffee. It's a conversation, not a form.
  • Be honest about energy drains. The boring, annoying stuff is where AI earns its keep fastest.
  • Read your profile afterwards. If something's wrong, say "that's not quite right…" and it'll fix it. You have the final word.
  • Peek at the catalog after Round 1 if you're curious. You can tick what appeals and paste your picks into Round 2.
