A head start for working with AI

Give your AI a place to live, a memory of you, and a plan.

Most people use AI like a brilliant stranger: every chat starts from zero. This kit turns it into an assistant that knows your life, follows your rules, and gets a little better every week. That goes for your personal life, your family, your home and your work, whatever you do.

Your path

No weekend marathons. Small, finished steps.

  1. Day 1 · Set up (about an hour)Install Obsidian, open the kit's vault folder, connect your AI tool, and say hello. Setup guide
  2. Week 1 · Get to know each other (4 × 20 min)The AI interviews you in rounds: your landscape, where help would feel good, deep dives, and how you like to work. It writes your profile, and you approve it. How the interview works
  3. Weeks 2–4 · First winsThree things you'll actually use, picked from your answers. Maybe a weekly review, a meal planner, a meeting-prep helper. See the menu
  4. Month 2+ · Grow it, at your paceConnect your calendar or email (read-only first), add small scripts, put things on a schedule. Each step comes from the harness plan. How the plan works
The only sentence you need to remember Open your AI in the vault and say "Continue my harness plan." It figures out where you are and what's next.

Before you start

💻

A computer

Mac, Windows or Linux. A phone is a nice extra, not required.

✨

An AI subscription

A paid plan of a capable assistant is strongly recommended. Agents that read and write files use a lot of capacity.

⏱️

20 minutes, now and then

That's the unit of progress. The plan remembers where you were.

Privacy, in one line Your notes stay on your computer. The AI reads the ones it needs for a task. You decide what goes in, and passwords and ID numbers never do. More on the setup page.
