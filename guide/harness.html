The harness plan

One file, _system/HARNESS-PLAN.md, that grows your setup from "Obsidian installed" to "things run by themselves", one small, proven step at a time. It's the memory that lets you stop, switch tools, and pick up weeks later.

The one sentence

Every time you have 15–30 minutes
Continue my harness plan.

That's it. The AI reads the plan, does the next step with you, proves it works, ticks it off, and leaves a note for next time.

What happens when you say it

  1. It loads lightlyReads the plan's Now table, the current phase and the last two log entries, plus your preferences. Not your whole vault.
  2. It picks the next stepThe first unticked step in the current phase, or the one you name.
  3. It says the plan in three linesWhat we'll do, how long, and what "done" looks like. It asks first if the step involves installing, signing up, paying or other people.
  4. It does it, smallIf the step is too big for one session, it splits it into sub-steps, writes them into the plan, and does the first.
  5. It proves itRuns the step's Check and shows you the evidence. No proof, no tick.
  6. It updates the planTicks the box, updates Now, adds a session-log entry and records any decisions with the why.
  7. It hands offThree lines: what changed, what's next, and the phrase to continue.

P0–P3 are the same for everyone. From P2.3 on, the interview fills the plan with your items, and steps that don't fit you get marked "not for me".

Anatomy of a step

- [ ] P0.3 Connect the AI to this vault and test it. Point the tool at this folder, then ask…
  Done when: it answers correctly and can create a test note.
  Check: ask it to create _inbox/hello.md, see it appear in Obsidian, then delete it yourself.
MarkMeans
[ ]To do
[/]In progress (started, or the check failed, with a note saying why)
[x]Done and verified
[-]Skipped / not for me (with the reason)
[?]Blocked: waiting on something, noted on the line

The parts of the file

📍

Now

Current phase, next step, anything blocked, last session. Glance here first.

✅

Phases P0–P7

Checklists with "done when" and "check" for every step.

💡

Personal backlog

Your ideas from the interview and later: value, effort (S/M/L), phase, status. ★ = do first.

⚖️

Decisions log

What was chosen and why, so nobody re-argues it in three months.

📓

Session log

Newest first: Did · Checked · Next · Notes. Any AI tool can continue from it alone.

Switching to a different AI tool
I'm continuing my AI setup in a new tool. Read AGENTS.md, then _system/HARNESS-PLAN.md (the Now table and the last two session-log entries), and tell me where we are and what's next. Don't change anything yet.

Tips

  • One step per session is a success. Twenty minutes a week gets you a working system in a couple of months.
  • Look at the file in Obsidian. The checkboxes are clickable, the log reads like a diary of your progress, and you can edit anything by hand.
  • Connections and automations are optional. Plenty of people get most of the value from P0–P3 plus a few skills.
  • Pair it with the weekly review. The review ends by offering "one harness step this week?"
  • When something breaks, say so. The step goes back to [/] with a note, and the next session starts there.
