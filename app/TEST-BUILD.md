**For testers only. This is not the finished app.** It's a small test window that checks whether the app's building blocks work on your computer. It takes about five minutes, and it doesn't change anything outside the folder you pick.

### 1. Download and open

- **Windows 10 or 11:** download `…-windows-x64.zip`, right-click it → **Extract All**, open the folder and double-click `ai-kickstart.exe`. Windows will say *"Windows protected your PC"* because this test build isn't signed yet: click **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Mac (Apple silicon, M1 or newer):** download `…-macos-arm64.zip`, double-click it, and open **AI Kickstart**. macOS will refuse the first time because it isn't signed yet: open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**, then open the app again.

### 2. Try the five checks

1. Click **Choose folder…** and pick a new, empty folder (make one on your Desktop).
2. In that folder, make a folder called `_inbox`, and inside it a text file called `hello.md` (Windows: Notepad → *Save as* → type `hello.md` and choose *All files*).
3. If you have [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md), click **Open in Obsidian**. No Obsidian? Skip this one.
4. Look at the window: each row shows ✓ when it worked.

### 3. Send back

A screenshot of the window, and one line: which Windows or macOS version, and anything that looked wrong or confusing.

To remove it afterwards, delete the app folder and the `.ai-kickstart` folder in your home folder (on Windows, `C:\Users\<you>\.ai-kickstart`).
